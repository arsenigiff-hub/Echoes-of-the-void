Integrated as the first-person repair viewmodel: PSX low-poly suit sleeves with graphite gloves, shown only during manual solar-panel and oxygen-system repairs. Repair actions use the source grab/push animations. The included texture is a recolored derivative of the source: exposed finger/palm pixels become graphite, exposed sleeve pixels become muted blue-grey, and the original folds are retained. A controlled unshaded material keeps the palette stable under EVA sunlight.
