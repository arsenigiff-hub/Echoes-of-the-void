# Medbay exam lamp

- Original low-poly model. Editable source: `blender/medbay_exam_lamp.blend`.
- The 256×256 albedo atlas is cropped, color-graded, and quantized from [Blue Metal Plate](https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_metal_plate) by Rob Tuytel, released under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
- The origin is at the center of the lamp foot at floor level. `BeamSocket` and `BeamTarget` place and aim the spotlight in `scripts/shuttle_blockout.gd`.
